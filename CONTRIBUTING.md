# Improve a case or its reference answer

Open an issue with the case ID, the relevant policy sentence and your reasoning. If two readings are defensible, explain the policy gap before proposing a new label. Model agreement alone is not an adjudication.

All current labels are unreviewed. Reviewers should record their decision and any proposed correction in the review worksheet. Keep related variants together when discussing a family, and check whether a policy clarification affects other cases. A report of a problem does not itself change the recorded review status.

Use fictional examples only. Do not submit customer records, personal information, credentials or proprietary support material. By submitting a contribution, you agree to license contributed data and documentation under CC BY 4.0 and contributed tools under MIT, as described in LICENSE.md. Confirm that you have the rights to share what you submit.

Run `node --test rich-score.test.mjs verify.test.mjs` before proposing tool changes. `checksums.json` covers released files; maintainers must regenerate affected hashes for an intentional change. Version changes to policy, inputs, labels or scoring rules. Keep published case results tied to the exact artifact and settings used to produce them.
